# Discovery, Connection & Belonging — Discovery Journeys

Companion to the philosophy doc
(`discovery-connection-belonging-v1.1.md`), the Phase 0 status doc
(`discovery-connection-belonging-status.md`), the location capture
model (`discovery-connection-belonging-location-model.md`) and the
Discovery Objects vocabulary
(`discovery-connection-belonging-objects.md`).

This document is intentionally experience-focused rather than
feature-focused. It does not define screens, navigation or
routes — it describes how people move through the Fresh Collective
world so that later design work can be measured against it.

Every discovery experience should support at least one of these
journeys and should never leave a member at a dead end.

---

## Purpose

This document describes the natural ways people discover Fresh
Collective.

It is intentionally experience-focused rather than feature-focused.

The goal is not to define screens or navigation, but to understand
how people move through the world and how each discovery naturally
leads to another opportunity for belonging.

Together with the Discovery Philosophy, Discovery Objects and
Location Model, these journeys form the foundation for Discovery,
Connection & Belonging.

---

## Design principles

### Discovery is a journey

People should never feel like they have reached the end of the
platform.

Every meaningful discovery should naturally lead to another.

### There is no single entry point

Every member arrives differently.

Some arrive through a place.

Others through a creator.

Others through an invitation.

Fresh Collective should support many natural beginnings.

### Discovery should reduce uncertainty

Every step should help someone answer:

- Is this for me?
- Do I belong here?
- What happens next?

### Discovery should build confidence

Each discovery should make the next step feel easier.

## Journey 1 — Discovering through Place

**Intent**

> "I'm looking for something near me."

**Journey**

```
Discover Places
       ↓
    Melbourne
       ↓
    The Grove
       ↓
Upcoming Gathering
       ↓
 Become a member
```

**Outcome**

The member discovers a local community that feels relevant and
welcoming.

> "I wasn't looking for this."

        ↓

 Another Collective

---

## Journey characteristics

Every journey should:

- begin with curiosity
- reduce uncertainty
- create confidence
- encourage participation
- naturally reveal another opportunity

Discovery is not about finding everything.

It is about finding the next meaningful step.

---

## Success

Members should feel that Fresh Collective is alive.

Every place they visit should reveal another opportunity to
belong.

Discovery should never feel like browsing a directory.

It should feel like exploring a world where every meaningful
connection opens the door to another.
