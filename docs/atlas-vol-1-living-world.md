### Where does your collective belong?

The guided creator ritual is short and clear. The creator:

- Chooses a **Location** (from The Atlas — the artwork of the collective is the Location's artwork)
- Chooses **Atmosphere** (exactly five words)
- Chooses a **Colour Palette** (the visual identity of the collective interface)
- Writes a **Collective Identity** (one sentence — the heart of the place)
- Writes a **Welcome Message** (the first thing visitors read)
- Sets **Practical details** (name, description, visibility, contribution)

# Chapter 8

# Design Principles

### The collective is the primary entity.

Everything belongs to a collective.

The Atlas simply provides the world in which collectives exist.

---

### We never build dashboards.

We build places.

---

### We never build feeds.

We build discoveries.

---

### We never build engagement loops.

We build reasons to return.

---

### We recommend because something fits.

Never because someone paid.

---

### We reward presence.

Never productivity.

---

### We encourage wandering.

Never rushing.

---

### We build for belonging.

Not addiction.

---

### Every place exists because it supports a human experience.

Nothing exists simply because it looks beautiful.

Every landmark should have a purpose that reflects how people naturally gather, learn, reflect, create or connect.

For example:

- Campfires invite people to gather.
- Paths encourage journeys.
- Harbours welcome new arrivals.
- Gardens represent nurturing and growth.
- Lookouts invite perspective.
- Libraries preserve shared knowledge.
- Rivers represent movement and flow.

The world is symbolic, but every symbol is rooted in a real human experience.

Our Language

We intentionally choose words that reinforce exploration, belonging and care.

The term **"your place"** is removed from the product language. Creators do not build places. Creators build **collectives**. Places already exist within the world.

# Chapter Nine

# The Atlas

*(new in v1.1)*

The Atlas is a curated part of the Fresh Collective world.

It is managed through the **Fresh Collective Admin Portal**.

Creators never manage the Atlas.

The Atlas contains **Locations**.

Each Location represents an island (or, in future, another type of place) within the world.

Locations are carefully curated to create a consistent visual language across the platform.

---

# Chapter Ten

# Locations

*(new in v1.1)*

A collective has **one Location**.

A Location provides:

- artwork
- atmosphere
- visual identity
- position within the world

A creator chooses where their collective lives.

Creators do not create Locations.

Fresh Collective curates them.

Multiple collectives may exist within the same Location.

---

# Chapter Eleven

# Collective Identity

*(v1.2 — Natural Elements removed; Location is now part of the identity)*

A collective's identity consists of:

- **Location** (chosen from The Atlas — where the collective lives)
- **Atmosphere** (five words)
- **Colour Palette** (drives the visual identity of the collective interface)
- **Identity Statement** (one sentence — the heart of the place)
- **Welcome Message** (the first thing visitors read)

These personalise the collective experience.

They are not used to procedurally generate artwork.

They:

- influence recommendations
- personalise onboarding
- shape copy
- inform future experiences

The **Colour Palette** additionally drives the visual colour language of the collective interface — buttons, links, accent colours, dividers, card highlights. It never changes the Location artwork.

---

# Chapter Twelve

# Artwork

*(v1.1 baseline; unchanged in v1.2)*

Island artwork is **curated**.

Fresh Collective owns and manages artwork through The Atlas.

Artwork belongs to **Locations**, never to individual collectives.

Artwork is **reusable** — multiple collectives may exist within the same Location.

Artwork should remain timeless, cohesive and recognisable as part of the Fresh Collective world.
