This is an experience design brief. It does not define screens,
routes, components, APIs or schemas. It describes the intended
member experience of Your World so that future product work can be
measured against it.

---

## Purpose

Your World is the member's personal home within Fresh Collective.

It is not:

- an admin dashboard
- an activity feed
- a productivity workspace

It is a place of reflection, orientation and gentle reconnection.

Members should leave Your World feeling grounded rather than
overwhelmed.

---

## Core question

Your World should quietly answer:

> "What is happening in my world today?"

Not:

> "What do I need to do?"

The distinction is deliberate. The moment Your World becomes a
task list is the moment it stops being restful.

---

## Philosophy

Discovery looks outward. Ways to Connect creates opportunities.
Your World looks inward.

It reflects the communities, places and experiences that have
become part of someone's life.

It should feel personal without feeling personalised by
algorithms. It should feel calm rather than busy. It should
encourage returning without demanding attention.

---

## Design principles

### Calm before busy

Information should be intentionally curated. Your World is not
another notification centre.

### Reflection before productivity

The experience should encourage awareness of one's communities
before presenting actions. Members should be able to arrive,
notice what is happening, and leave without having *done*
anything, if that is what they need.

### Human before metrics

No streaks. No contribution scores. No engagement rankings. No
"you're on a 3-week streak" nudges. Human indicators of a life in
communities, not scoreboards.

### Familiar before new

Prioritise the communities and experiences that already matter to
the member before surfacing new discoveries. Familiarity is
belonging in slow motion; Your World should honour it.

### Gentle invitations

The interface should offer opportunities rather than obligations.
Avoid language implying urgency unless it is genuinely present
(e.g. a Gathering in the next hour, a payment that has actually
failed). Everything else is a suggestion, not a task.

### Your World should evolve

The experience should naturally change as someone's journey
through Fresh Collective grows. A member with one Collective and
a first Pathway should see something calmly different from a
member with three Collectives and years of practice.

---

## The role of Your World

Your World supports experiences such as:

- seeing what has happened recently across the member's
  communities
- remembering where you left off in a Pathway or conversation
- reconnecting with communities that already matter
- noticing upcoming Gatherings without being nagged about them
- reflecting on the shape of a life across communities
- feeling welcomed back after time away

It does **not** support:

- assigning tasks
- chasing catch-up on missed activity
- ranking effort
- gating access behind participation

---

## Information architecture

Categories of information that belong on Your World. This is not a
layout — it is the taxonomy of what the surface should offer.

### Welcome

A warm, contextual welcome — often personal but never contrived.
"Welcome back" rather than "You have 12 unread notifications."

### Recent Moments

Meaningful moments from the member's communities. These are not
notifications and they do not carry counts. They help someone feel
connected to what has happened while they were away. Recent Moments
is already a first-class surface elsewhere in the platform; on Your
World it appears in service of orientation, not as an inbox.

### Continue Your Journey

Things already in progress:

- Pathways with a next step waiting
- reflections partially written
- upcoming Gatherings the member has booked

The framing is invitational ("continue when you're ready"), never
demanding ("you're behind").

### Around Your World

Meaningful happenings across the member's Collectives — the wider
sense of "what's alive." Not every event. Only the ones likely to
matter to this particular member, curated through human editorial
signal rather than engagement optimisation. Where Recent Moments
looks back at what happened, Around Your World looks around at
what's active right now.

### Gentle Invitations

Occasional opportunities beyond the member's existing world:

- an upcoming Gathering in a familiar Collective
- a new Collective nearby
- an online experience that matches an existing interest
- an invitation from a Creator

These should never feel like assignments. If a member ignores
every invitation for a month, Your World should have nothing to
say about it.

---

## Relationship to Atlas

The Atlas represents the wider Fresh Collective world.

Your World represents the member's lived experience within it.

The two are complementary: the Atlas is the map of possibility;
Your World is the map of what has become real for one person.

---

## Relationship to Discovery

Discovery expands someone's world.

Your World reflects the world they have already built.

A member exploring Discover Places is looking outward; a member
returning to Your World is coming home. Both are valid arrivals
into Fresh Collective, at different moments in the same
relationship.

---

## Relationship to Ways to Connect

Ways to Connect helps create meaningful moments.

Your World helps those moments feel remembered.

A Gathering attended, a Pathway continued, a conversation joined,
a Collective returned to — these become part of the quiet story
Your World reflects back.

---

## What does not belong

Explicitly out of scope for Your World, no matter how tempting:

- notification overload
- productivity metrics
- unread counts on every surface
- leaderboards
- contribution rankings
- endless scrolling feeds
- pressure to "catch up"
- social-media-style engagement loops
- streaks and daily-goal mechanics
- "you missed this" framing

Any design proposal that would introduce one of the above should
be examined with unusual care — probably rejected, occasionally
reshaped into something calmer.

---

## Success

Someone opening Your World should feel:

- welcomed
- grounded
- reconnected
- gently informed
- encouraged

Not:

- overwhelmed
- behind
- guilty
- distracted
- pressured

If Your World consistently produces the second column for any
member, the design has failed regardless of what any metric says.

---

## Future evolution

Your World should evolve as the member's relationship with Fresh
Collective deepens. Conceptual rather than prescriptive:

- **Early days.** The welcome is prominent. The member's first
  Collective sits at the centre. First Pathway if they've started
  one. Gentle invitations lean toward orientation — "here's what
  else exists" — rather than deep continuity.
- **Established.** The rhythms of the member's practice are
  visible. Recent Moments and Around Your World carry more of the
  weight; the welcome recedes. Continue Your Journey knows where
  the member habitually returns. Invitations lean toward what a
  regular practitioner would find meaningful.
- **Long-standing.** Continuity becomes visible in a way that only
  time can produce: traditions, recurring gatherings, communities
  the member has been part of for years. Your World reflects the
  shape of a life across communities without ever pinning that
  shape into a metric.

The evolution should be felt, not announced. There is no "level
up" moment. Your World simply looks more like the member's life as
that life takes shape.
