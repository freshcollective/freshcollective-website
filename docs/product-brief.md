# Product Brief — Fresh Collective

## What Fresh Collective Is

Fresh Collective is a membership-based transformation platform for women moving from survival, stuckness, and over-functioning into expansion that actually lasts.

It is a **structured transformation system.**

A member should enter in the right place, follow a clear path, feel connected to the community, and grow in a way their nervous system can actually hold.

## What Fresh Collective Is NOT

- Not a course library
- Not a traditional coaching portal
- Not a random content vault
- Not a high-touch 1:1 coaching container

## Core Platform Idea

The platform has **one foundation**, **multiple deepening pathways**, and a **live community layer** holding it all together.

The founder's role is to be the architect of the system. She designs the foundational work, builds the core pathways, and may later curate or integrate other contributors. Everything sits inside the Fresh Collective structure.

---

## The Four Design Principles

### 1. Simple, short, no overwhelm
People do not need more information. They need less, delivered well. Content should be short, digestible, and designed to shift behaviour — not to impress people.

The platform's quality comes from how usable it is, not how comprehensive it is.

### 2. Community is the value
The deepest value is connection: live calls, message groups, push notifications, being in the room together, and shared reflection.

Content is the structure. Community is the heart.

### 3. Behaviour change over information delivery
Every lesson, pathway, prompt, and live moment should support integration and lived change.

### 4. A member's week should feel connected
The platform should help members know what to focus on now, what live/community moment is happening, and how to take one practical step.

## Platform Areas

### 1. START HERE: REAL Journey

REAL Journey is the foundational experience and the gravitational centre of the platform.

REAL stands for:
- **R**ecognise
- **E**xplore
- **A**lign
- **L**ead

It is the first place a member starts. It should feel guided, structured, short, practical, calming, and stabilising.

REAL Journey is not a one-time course someone finishes and leaves behind. Members can return to it at any time.

Pathways, live calls, prompts, and community moments all reference back to REAL.

**Outcome:** stabilisation, self-awareness, nervous system safety, forward movement, shared language across the platform.

Do not make REAL Journey feel heavy. Keep it bite-sized and easy to move through.

---

### 2. THE HEART: Live Layer

This is the primary value of the membership.

Includes:
- One monthly live call
- Community space / group chat
- Push notification prompts
- Integration threads after calls
- Themed community moments

The Live Layer is central — not an add-on. It should be built early.

**Boundaries:**
- The founder is not available 24/7
- This is not high-touch coaching
- The structure holds the community
- Do not build features that create founder burnout

---

### 3. THE ROOMS: Pathways

Pathways are where members go deeper after REAL Journey.

Three top-level pathways based on **The Natural Leader Model:**

| Pathway | Rooms |
|---|---|
| **Growth** | Self-awareness, Self-trust, Uniqueness |
| **Transformation** | Embodiment, Vision, Purpose |
| **Essence** | Harmony, Creativity, Manifestation |

**For v1: Build Growth only.** Transformation and Essence appear as "Coming Soon."

Navigation should feel simple on the surface, with depth underneath.

---

### 4. COMMUNITY

Community is the heart of the platform. Members should feel part of something alive, not just logging into a content library.

Includes:
- Community posts
- Prompts
- Live call discussion threads
- Integration check-ins
- Member reflections

Keep it clean and simple. Do not overbuild.

---

## Entry Paths

There are two valid entry paths into the platform.

**Path 1: REAL Journey → Membership**
Someone buys REAL Journey as a low-cost entry product, completes it, then is invited into the full membership.

**Path 2: Membership → REAL Journey**
Someone joins the full membership and begins with REAL Journey as "Start Here" before moving into The Rooms and The Heart.

The platform must support both paths.

## Version 1 Priorities

1. Beautiful member dashboard
2. REAL Journey structure (4 phases, lessons, reflections, progress)
3. The Heart / Live Layer area
4. Growth Pathway (3 rooms)
5. Simple community area
6. Authentication-ready structure (Supabase)
7. Clear navigation
8. Mobile-responsive design

## What NOT to Build in v1

- Complex gamification
- Badges or achievement systems
- Excessive course library features
- Complicated admin systems (unless genuinely needed)
- Anything that makes the platform feel overwhelming
- Transformation and Essence pathways (Coming Soon only)
